We start by immersing ourselves in your business. We map your current processes, interview your key stakeholders, and identify exactly where time and revenue are being lost. 02✓
Week 1–2
Strategy & transformation roadmap

We co-create a phased roadmap aligned to your goals, budget, and timeline — prioritising the highest-impact changes first. You get full visibility into what we're building, why we're building it, and what success looks like at every stage. No surprises. No scope creep.

Phased roadmap Technology selection Budget planning Success metrics

Week 2–5
Implementation & integration

Our certified consultants configure your CRM and ERP, migrate your data, and integrate all connected systems. We work in structured sprints with weekly check-ins so you always know exactly where things stand. Your operations continue uninterrupted while we build in the background.

We train every user on their specific workflows — not generic software training. On go-live day, we're on-site or online with your team to make sure every person is confident and the system performs exactly as designed. We don't hand over documentation and disappear. We stay until it's working.

Role-based training Go-live support UAT testing Documentation
